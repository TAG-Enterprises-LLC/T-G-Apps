Let It Ride

Table poker against the pay table

Place three equal bets, see three cards, and pull back up to two bets as the two community cards are revealed. A pair of tens or better pays, up to 1,000 to 1 for a royal flush.

Caribbean Stud

Table poker against the dealer

Ante, look at five cards and one dealer card, then fold or raise. The dealer must qualify with ace-king or better, and big hands pay up to 100 to 1 on the raise.
